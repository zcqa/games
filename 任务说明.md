1. In a realistic temperate forest clearing, the player must gather enough wood and stone before nightfall to craft, place, and light a campfire.
2. In a realistic temperate forest clearing, the player must first collect loose branches and loose stones by hand, use them to craft an axe and a pickaxe, use the axe to harvest timber and the pickaxe to mine rock, and then use the gathered timber and rock to craft, place, and use a shelter before nightfall.
3. In a realistic temperate forest clearing, the player must collect loose branches and loose stones by hand, use them to craft an axe and a pickaxe, use the axe to harvest timber and the pickaxe to mine rock, and then use the gathered timber and rock to establish a complete overnight camp with a placed usable shelter and a placed lit campfire before nightfall.
4. In a realistic snowbound abandoned mountain camp, the player must search the camp for fuel and ignition supplies and use them to restore the heating stove.
5. In a realistic snowbound abandoned mountain camp, the player must find a replacement battery, salvage copper wire from damaged equipment, use both items to repair the radio, and then use the repaired radio to send a rescue message.
6. In a realistic snowbound abandoned mountain camp, the player must recover a repair toolkit from a snow-blocked shed, collect the missing radio components from scattered camp debris, use the toolkit and components to repair the radio and a portable antenna assembly, carry the repaired antenna to the marked elevated site, place it there, and then transmit a rescue signal.
7. At a realistic abandoned desert expedition site surrounded by dunes and exposed rock, the player must collect salvaged shelter-material units from scattered expedition debris, use those material units to craft a shade shelter, and place the completed shelter.
8. At a realistic abandoned desert expedition site surrounded by dunes and exposed rock, the player must collect salvaged construction-material units from scattered expedition debris, use those material units to craft and place a shade shelter and a functional solar water still, and then operate the placed water still to produce drinking water.
9. At a realistic abandoned desert supply station surrounded by dunes and exposed rock, the player must restore the damaged solar array, repair the damaged water pump, use the restored systems to produce clean drinking water, collect shelter-material units from scattered station debris, and use those material units to craft, place, and use a shelter.
10. In a realistic rural village in a grassy valley, the player starts with three trade tokens and must spend one trade token to purchase raw meat from the market, then use the village cooking pot to prepare a cooked meal.
11. In a realistic rural village in a grassy valley, the central water well is broken, and the player must collect well-repair material units from discarded workshop supplies, use those material units to craft a well repair component, install the component to restore the well, and then draw drinking water from the restored well.
12. In a realistic rural village in a grassy valley, the player starts with exactly three trade tokens. At the village market, the player must spend exactly three trade tokens to purchase exactly one cooking ingredient bundle. At the village cooking pot, the player must consume exactly one cooking ingredient bundle to prepare cooked provisions. The player must also collect timber from fallen logs and stone from loose rock piles. The player must use timber and stone to craft exactly one campfire kit, and separately use timber and stone to craft exactly one shelter kit. At the marked public supply camp site, the player must place the campfire kit to create a lit campfire and place the shelter kit to create a usable shelter.
